About This Project

Beyond the Game is a digital journalism project examining important developments in professional sport. Through research, data analysis and interactive visualisations, the project explores how technology, competition and commercial expansion influence athlete safety and welfare.

This project brings together written journalism and digital storytelling to make complex sporting issues accessible to a wider audience.
